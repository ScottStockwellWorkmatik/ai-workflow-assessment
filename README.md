# AI Workflow Assessment

Workmatik tool for choosing which marketing workflows to improve with AI first. Pick from 31 starter workflows built from the AI Roles Assessment activities (every role appears in at least one), load your roles scope from the AI Roles Assessment, add your own steps and workflows. Score each on value, feasibility and risk (seven questions, 1 to 5), set an indicative EU AI Act tier, and get a ranked shortlist, a value and feasibility chart and the use cases to start with.

Runs entirely in the browser. Nothing is sent or stored. Download results as XLSX, load them back to continue, and compare earlier assessments.

Live address: https://scottstockwellworkmatik.github.io/ai-workflow-assessment/

The questions, scoring, thresholds, tiers and starter workflows are the author's draft judgement and have no published source. The EU AI Act tier is indicative, not legal advice.

## Shared IDs
Workflows are WF01 to WF31 (your own: WFC01 onwards). Activities use the AI Roles Assessment IDs (A001 onwards, custom AC001 onwards). The Excel download includes a "Workmatik data" sheet so other Workmatik tools can match on these IDs. Files from the earlier version (W01 to W15) still load.
